Fixes #4087.

`aggregateTiming` estimated each side of the comparison with a **mean over 15 repetitions**. Host contention can only ever *add* time to a measurement, never remove it, so the mean converges on the query's cost **plus the runner's average interference**, and one preempted repetition out of fifteen moves it. The two sides run in separate loops at different moments, so a burst landing in one and not the other tilts the ratio directly.

That failed PR #4076 twice in a row — a change to `cmd/goobers/remediationcheckpoint.go` that touches nothing in this package:

```
small corpus (900 rows, NOT asserted): aggregate 1.984833ms vs window 2.007391ms (1.01x)
large corpus (4500 rows): aggregate 6.554497ms vs window 8.145425ms (1.24x)
--- FAIL: TestLatestPerWorkflowScalesBetterThanTheWindowFunction
```

A 1.01x reading on the small corpus is the tell: at 900 rows the two queries were indistinguishable through the noise.

## Change

Time each repetition individually and keep the **fastest** for each side. Same reps, same corpora, same claim, same 1.5x floor — an estimator a loaded host cannot inflate.

Locally, three consecutive runs:

```
large corpus (4500 rows): aggregate 816.959µs vs window 3.295ms  (4.03x)
large corpus (4500 rows): aggregate 860.5µs   vs window 3.13475ms (3.64x)
large corpus (4500 rows): aggregate 823.708µs vs window 3.288292ms (3.99x)
```

Back in the 3.0x–5.7x band the test's own header records, with the small corpus now reading a stable 1.59x–1.74x instead of 1.01x.
