# Carry the auditor filing threshold through telemetry wire conversions (#7123)

The growth is the existing fault-audit conversions in `cmd/goobers` copying
the two new fields, `FaultFinding.Fileable` and
`FaultAuditReport.FilingThreshold`,
between `creditgraph` and `telemetryclient`. Those conversions already live
in `telemetrydefectplane.go` and `telemetryquery.go`; leaving the fields out
would silently drop them from the defect-plane and `telemetry-query` outputs.
The threshold derivation and eval publication live in `internal/creditgraph`
and `internal/readservice`.
