# HAW contained parent custody composition

The dedicated pod supervisor now preserves cancellation transcripts and reports
adapter-observed usage. `cmd/goobers` also composes the authenticated parent
workflow-grant client into its existing pod harness constructor. Its endpoint,
run and contract are trusted launch inputs; grants are fetched at invocation and
revoked at exit, never retained in execution kits. The run-owned bounded storage,
contract ownership index, transport and containment remain in `internal/childpod`
and `internal/dispatcher`. No new CLI command or daemon Kubernetes access is added.
