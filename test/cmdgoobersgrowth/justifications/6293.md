# Exempt curation claims from the repeated no-work circuit breaker

The no-work circuit breaker (#5379) counted every completed single-item run,
so a `curate-resweep` run that correctly confirmed a `goobers:ready` item
needed no change stripped its readiness and parked it as
`goobers:needs-human` (#6293). The growth records the claim's declared
selection purpose (`curation` for `backlog-query --claim` with
`curation: "true"` or `--resweep`) on the existing item-repo annotation
written in `cmd/goobers/itemrepository.go`, and has `applyNoWorkStreak` skip
curation claims. Both the claim-side recording and the breaker already live in
`cmd/goobers`, so the few lines stay beside them; the annotation fold in
`internal/instanceannotations` carries the new field.
