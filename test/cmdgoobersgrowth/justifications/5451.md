# Fix #5451: drop provider claim markers that outlive their ledger lease

Growth comes from the backlog claim session in `cmd/goobers`:

- **`confirmProviderClaim` and `reconfirmLease`** (`claimobservation.go`).
  After the provider claim marker is written, the session re-reads its lease
  under the claims lock. If the lease is expired, released, or replaced, it
  retracts its marker and returns `ledgerLeaseLostError`; if the retraction
  fails, the stage fails. This is backlog-query stage policy (which lease the
  stage claimed, which marker it wrote), so it belongs with the rest of that
  stage's claim flow.
- **`dropLostLease`** (`backlogquery.go`). This releases this run's own lapsed
  entry, removes the item from the session's claimed sets, and records a
  ledger refusal. The bookkeeping it changes lives in this package.
- **`claimEntryWire`** (`writeplanes.go`, +1 line). This forwards the new
  `RenewedAt` field to the claims plane.

The ledger change (renewals keep `ClaimedAt` and record `RenewedAt`) is in
`internal/localscheduler`, not here.
