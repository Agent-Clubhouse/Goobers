Issue #5897 makes the daemon root-identity check that guards every daemon-API
mutation command (`run`, `run cancel`, `run abort`, `approve`, `override`,
`rerun-stage`, and `escalations resolve`) wait while a restarting daemon answers
that it has not finished starting, instead of failing at once with an error
that reads like a broken identity. The added lines recognize the daemon's two
"not ready yet" answers, bound the wait by the caller's deadline or a fallback,
and word the operator-facing note and error.

They belong in cmd/goobers because the check they extend (remoteroot.go) and
the startup placeholder whose reply they recognize (startup_api.go) both live
there, and the check is shared only by this command's mutation paths. The wait
loop itself is not new code: it reuses internal/retryutil.
