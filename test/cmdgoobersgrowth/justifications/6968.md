# #6968: name the claims-lock holder when a waiter times out

Growth: +17 non-test lines and +0 files in `cmd/goobers`, all in
`providercmd.go`.

- `claimsLockTimeoutError` gains the holder fields and a constructor that reads
  the holder record. Both timeout returns in `acquireClaimLock` now go through
  it, and `Error()` appends the holder when one is known.
- `acquireClaimLock` announces the acquiring operation (one call).
- `recordClaimLockTimeout` adds `holderOperation`, `holderPid` and
  `holderHeldFor` to the `claims_lock_timeout` journal event.

## Could any of it live elsewhere?

The mechanism (the sidecar record, `Handle.Announce`, `ReadHolder` and the
clear-on-release) is in `internal/platform/lock`. What remains here is the
claims-lock error type and its journal event, which already live in this
package.
