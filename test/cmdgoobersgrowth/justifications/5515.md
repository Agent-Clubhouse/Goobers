# #5515: dashboard waits for a daemon still in first-boot startup

Growth: +12 non-test lines and +0 files in `cmd/goobers`, all in
`dashboard.go`.

`goobers dashboard` gave up on a running daemon after a fixed 30s attach
timeout, so a first-boot daemon that was still answering its "daemon is
starting" 503 made the dashboard exit and the installer's dashboard step fail.
The wait now feeds the daemon's advertised startup budget and progress into
`startuphint.Bound`. The growth is the call site (creating the bound,
observing 503 hints, checking its deadline), a cap variable, and two help-text
lines.

## Could any of it live elsewhere?

The bounding policy already lives outside this package: it moved from
`internal/workerblob` into `internal/startuphint` so the resident worker
and the dashboard share one implementation. What remains is the dashboard's
own attach loop, which is in `cmd/goobers` with the rest of the command.
